## Research

- **Introspecting the Alignment Side Effects of Fine-Tuning** (arXiv 2608.04347, preprint). Fine-tuning on a task unrelated to safety can degrade the alignment a base model had. We train a model to describe these shifts and propose DAIA, an introspection adapter that reads both base-model activations and the activation differences fine-tuning induced.
- **Orth-Dion: Eliminating Geometric Mismatch in Distributed Low-Rank Spectral Optimization** (CoLoRAI Workshop, ICML 2026). Dion normalizes columns in a way that misses the polar geometry Muon targets, which costs a $\sqrt{r}$ factor in the rate. Swapping that single line for QR orthogonalization recovers Muon-matching rates at the same communication budget.
- **On Fairness of Task Arithmetic: The Role of Task Vectors** (ICLR 2026, supervised by Hiroki Naganuma at ProPlace). A $\lambda$-scaled merge of subgroup-specific task vectors keeps accuracy while lowering DPD and EOD relative to full fine-tuning and LoRA, with a theoretical DPD upper bound linking the coefficients to subgroup vector norms.
- **Which Geometry on Which Layer? A Principled Criterion for Mixed-Optimizer Training** (under review).
- **The Long Delay to Arithmetic Generalization: When Learned Representations Outrun Behavior** (under review).

More projects and the full list of papers are on my [website](https://lauragomezjurado.github.io/).
